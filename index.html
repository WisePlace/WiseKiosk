WiseKiosk

Turn any Android phone or tablet into a locked-down, single-purpose kiosk screen — showing exactly one webpage, fullscreen, and nothing else.

Download WiseKiosk.apk
Android · sideloaded APK · not on the Play Store

What it does

Fullscreen, locked

Pins one webpage to the screen — no status bar, no nav buttons, no way out without your say-so.

Any resolution

Auto-fit the device's own screen, or lock to a fixed resolution and it'll scale to fit.

Password-protected settings

Set a password so only you can get back into setup — everyone else just sees the kiosk page.

Starts on its own

Launches straight into kiosk mode on boot — plug the device in and it's ready, no unlocking needed.

How it works

Install the APK

Download it below and open it on the device — Android will ask to allow installs from your browser the first time.

Enter your URL

Type the page you want displayed, and test it right from setup before committing to it.

Tune it to your setup

Optionally set a resolution, a settings password, and whether it should auto-launch on boot.

Start kiosk mode

That's it — the page takes over the screen. Swipe down from the top edge any time for a quick menu to reload or reopen settings.

Note: since WiseKiosk isn't distributed through the Play Store, Android will flag it as an app from an "unknown source" the first time you install it. That's expected for sideloaded apps — just allow it for your browser when prompted.
